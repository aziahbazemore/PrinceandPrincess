Dear Friend,

We established this closet in 1980 at 1404 Wisconsin Avenue NW with a simple goal: to build a comfortable room where people could gather, sit on a quality leather couch, share stories, and find clothing that carries a soul.

Over the last 45 years, we’ve styled scholars, neighborhood residents, and sneaker purists. Every track jacket and handsewn loafer in this space was hand-selected because it represents timeless design and comfort. We believe fashion should feel cozy, familiar, and slightly imperfect—just like home.

If you're in the neighborhood, drop in. The couch is ready, the lights are low, and we might just pour you a glass of fine bourbon.

With warmth,
The Prince & Princess Family
